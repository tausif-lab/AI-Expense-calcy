# ECONOMICS.md

## Converted Lead Value

The value of a converted lead for Credex depends on how much AI infrastructure spend the customer moves through Credex credits.

For this estimate, I assumed the typical successful customer is:

* a startup with 10–30 employees
* spending roughly $2,000–$10,000/month on AI tools and APIs

If Credex captures even 15–20% margin on discounted infrastructure credits, a customer spending:

* $5,000/month
  could generate:
* ~$750–$1,000/month gross profit

Assuming the average retained customer stays for 12 months, the estimated customer lifetime value becomes:

```text id="jlwm5m"
$800/month × 12
= ~$9,600 LTV
```

Even with rough assumptions, each successfully converted infrastructure customer is likely worth several thousand dollars annually.

## Estimated CAC By Channel

The early GTM strategy focuses primarily on organic founder and developer communities, keeping CAC relatively low.

| Channel                    | Estimated CAC |
| -------------------------- | ------------- |
| Hacker News                | $10–$25       |
| Reddit founder communities | $15–$40       |
| X/Twitter founder audience | $20–$50       |
| Direct founder outreach    | $40–$80       |
| Indie Hackers              | $15–$30       |

These acquisition costs remain low because the product itself creates shareable audit reports that function as viral content.

The largest cost is likely founder outreach time rather than infrastructure or ad spend.

## Conversion Funnel Assumptions

A realistic funnel could look like:

```text id="jlwm5n"
1,000 landing visitors
→ 250 completed audits (25%)
→ 80 email captures (32%)
→ 20 Credex consultations booked (25%)
→ 5 infrastructure purchases (25%)
```

If:

* 5 customers convert
* each customer is worth ~$9,600 annually

then:

```text id="jlwm5o"
5 × $9,600
= ~$48,000 ARR
```

from every ~1,000 targeted visitors.

This becomes profitable quickly because customer acquisition costs remain relatively low compared to infrastructure customer value.

## Path To $1M ARR In 18 Months

To reach $1M ARR:

```text id="jlwm5p"
$1,000,000 ÷ $9,600
≈ 105 active customers
```

That means Credex would need roughly:

* 100–120 retained infrastructure customers

within 18 months.

Using the earlier funnel assumptions:

```text id="jlwm5q"
5 customers per 1,000 targeted visitors
```

Credex would need approximately:

```text id="jlwm5r"
20,000–25,000 highly targeted visitors
```

over 18 months.

For founder-focused SaaS infrastructure products, this is realistic if:

* audit reports become widely shared
* founder communities adopt the tool
* public audit links generate organic distribution
* Credex consistently surfaces meaningful savings opportunities

---

## What Must Be True For This To Work

Several things must be true for the product to become a meaningful acquisition channel.

First, the audit recommendations must feel financially credible. If users feel the tool is generating fake savings, trust collapses immediately.

Second, the public share pages must create organic distribution loops. The product works best if founders share screenshots and report links publicly.

Third, Credex must consistently offer savings meaningful enough to justify operational switching costs. Small savings are unlikely to drive consultations, but larger API infrastructure savings can justify deeper conversations.

Finally, the product must position itself as operational infrastructure rather than “another AI tool.” The strongest long-term advantage is becoming the default way startups benchmark and optimize AI spending.
