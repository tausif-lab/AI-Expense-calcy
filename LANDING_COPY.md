# FAQ

## 1. What does this tool actually do?

The audit analyzes your AI subscriptions, plans, API usage, and team setup to identify unnecessary spending, duplicated tooling, and cheaper alternatives.

---

## 2. Which AI tools are supported?

The audit currently supports Cursor, ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Gemini, Windsurf, OpenAI API, and Anthropic API plans.

---

## 3. Do I need to create an account?

No. You can generate an audit instantly without creating an account or logging in.

---

## 4. How accurate are the savings estimates?

Savings estimates are based on current public pricing data, plan comparisons, seat utilization, and deterministic audit rules. Recommendations are designed to be financially explainable rather than AI-generated guesses.

---

## 5. Why does the report ask for my email?

Email is optional and only used if you want your audit report link delivered to your inbox or want future optimization alerts for your AI stack.
